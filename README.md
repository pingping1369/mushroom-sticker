# Mushroom Sticker · 野生菌贴纸库

Free, realistic **wild mushroom stickers**: transparent PNG, white outline, one sticker per growth stage (young / mature), plus natural clusters for some species.
Browse, search and download them at **[mushroomsticker.com](https://mushroomsticker.com)**.

写实风格的**野生菌贴纸**：透明底、白边，每种菌按幼菇和成熟各一张，部分物种另有自然丛生版。在 [mushroomsticker.com](https://mushroomsticker.com) 浏览和下载。

**46 stickers · 25 species** — licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

> ⚠️ Illustrations for enjoyment and record only. Never use them to decide whether a mushroom is edible.
> 插画仅供欣赏和记录，不能用来判断能不能吃。

## Files

- `stickers/<species>/<species>-<stage>.png` — transparent PNG; `<stage>` is `young`, `mature` or a composition such as `cluster`.
- `stickers.json` — every sticker with its species names, family, stage, page on mushroomsticker.com and the source of the reference photo it was drawn from.
